# TrustThread Demo Knowledge Base

## Leave and Working Hours
Employees may request leave through the HR portal. Standard working hours are 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Friday.

## Security Incident Reporting
Security incidents must be reported immediately to the security team. The process is identify, contain, investigate, remediate and document. Preserve evidence and log every decision.

## AI Usage
Do not disclose passwords, API keys, customer confidential information or restricted documents to AI systems. Suspicious instructions should be reported.

## Secrets Handling
API keys and credentials must be stored in an approved secret manager. Never paste credentials into an AI prompt or public chat.
